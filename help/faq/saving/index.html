Saving

What does the status mean?​

Saving means the page is sending what you typed. Saved means it is stored. Offline (will retry) means this page will send it when the connection returns. Leave the page open. Conflict means the entry was saved somewhere else, and your wording is still in the box. Error means it could not be saved, often because the note is blank or no diary is checked.

I closed the page before it said Saved​

On your own phone or computer, reopen the editor. If a newer copy is still in this browser, the page offers to restore it. That copy lasts 14 days, and signing out removes it. On a shared device, the browser does not keep a copy. If the page was still open, it retries after the connection returns.

It says Conflict​

The box still has what you typed. Copy your text keeps that wording. Load the saved entry shows the wording that was saved and replaces the box. Saving does not overwrite the other copy on its own.

I am on someone else's computer​

Check This is a shared device before you sign in. That session does not keep a copy in the browser. Choose Sign out when you are done. Signing out also removes copies this browser kept from another session.

Getting started

Someone who runs Pebble Diary creates your account and sends you an email. The email has a link to set your password. The link works once and expires after one hour. If it has expired, ask for a new one from the sign-in page.

Sign in with your email and password.

  • Leave This is a shared device unchecked on a phone or computer that is yours. You stay signed in for 14 days, and each visit starts that 14 days again.
  • Check This is a shared device on a computer other people use. You are signed out when the browser closes, and that browser does not keep an unsaved copy.

Sign out is in the header. Use it when you are done on a shared computer.

If you forget the password, use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page. Signing in answers the usual problems. Your name, email, time zone, and password are on Settings.

A new account has one private diary. After you sign in you see today. A shared diary is separate from that private one. Sharing explains how to create one, invite someone, change a role, or leave. Day explains the calendar and the two ways to read a day. Diaries in the header is where you see your diary, add another, and choose the defaults for a new entry. Diaries explains that page. Write is where you save an entry. Write explains that page.

Privacy in the footer explains who can read what you write, and what the service can read. Privacy is that page.

Write

Open Write in the header.

Type in the box. The page saves on its own after you pause, and it shows Saving, then Saved. What you save is kept as you wrote it, including line breaks. A blank note is not saved.

Date is optional. Leave it blank and the entry uses today in the time zone on Settings. Or pick the day the entry is about. That can be an earlier day.

Diaries start from the ones you checked on Diaries. Check at least one. A shared diary has the word Shared beside its name. The same entry can be in more than one diary, and it is still stored once. People in a shared diary can read an entry you put there. They cannot read an entry you leave only in a private diary. On an entry you already saved, uncheck one diary and check another to move it. That does not change who currently owns it.

Transfer this entry offers an entry you wrote to someone else. They have to accept. Sharing explains that handoff.

Save entry saves immediately. You can also wait for Saved. Day is where you read it again. Write for this day opens this page with that date filled in. Leave the date blank on a new entry to use today. On an entry you already saved, leave the date blank to keep the saved day. Edit on the Raw view opens an entry you wrote.

If the connection drops, the status says Offline (will retry). Leave the page open. When the connection returns, it saves. On a phone or computer that is yours, a copy also stays in this browser until the save is confirmed, you sign out, or 14 days pass. If you reopen the editor and that copy is newer, the page offers to restore it. On a shared device, this browser does not keep that copy. Saving answers the usual questions.

If you leave the page before it says Saved, the browser asks you to confirm.

If the entry was saved somewhere else, the status says Conflict. What you typed stays in the box. Load the saved entry shows what was saved. Copy your text copies what you typed so you can keep it.

Delete entry, on an entry you wrote, removes it from every diary it is in. On someone else's entry, an Owner or an Editor sees Remove from and the diary's name. That takes the entry out of that diary. If it is also in another diary, it stays there. If that was its only diary, it is deleted.

You can change the wording of an entry you wrote. Someone else cannot change that wording.

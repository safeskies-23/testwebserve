Day

After you sign in, you see today.

Previous month and Next month change the calendar and keep the day you are reading. Today comes back to today. While the day changes, the page says Loading. A dot on a date means that day has an entry. The dot is a mark, not a color by itself, and the date's name also says that it has entries.

Days with entries lists those days, newest first. Choose one to open it.

Diary reads that day's entries one after another, in the order they were saved. The words stay as you wrote them. Raw shows each entry on its own, with the time, the diaries it is in, and Edit when you wrote it. Switching views keeps the same day. You can copy the address of either view.

All diaries shows every diary you can read. A shared diary has the word Shared beside its name. Choose one name to hide the others for this day, the calendar, and the list. An entry that is in two of your diaries still appears once. An entry in a shared diary shows the name of the person who wrote it. An entry that is only in someone else's private diary is not listed, and its day is not marked.

A day with nothing written says so. Write for this day opens the editor with that date filled in. Write explains saving.

Delete entry on an entry you wrote removes it from every diary it is in, then returns you to that day. On someone else's entry, Remove from takes it out of that one diary and leaves it in any other diary it belongs to.

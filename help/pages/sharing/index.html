Sharing

Open Diaries in the header.

Create diary adds a diary you own. Leave Shared diary unchecked and it stays private, like your personal diary. Check it when other people should be able to read it. The word Shared appears beside that diary. A private diary cannot be invited to.

Members is on each shared diary. It lists who is in the diary and whether they are an Owner, an Editor, or a Contributor. A person who has deleted their account stays on the list as a closed account until the account is erased. Their entries in this diary still show their name.

An Owner can invite an Editor or a Contributor. An Editor can invite a Contributor. Enter their email and choose Send invitation. The page says the same thing whether or not that email already has an account. If they are already in the diary, it says so.

The email contains a link. The link works once and expires after 7 days. Sending again to the same email for the same diary replaces the pending invitation, and the old link stops working. Revoke cancels a pending invitation. An Editor can revoke a Contributor invitation. An Owner can revoke either.

If the email already has an account, that person signs in and chooses Accept. If it does not, the link asks for a name and a password, creates the account, and joins the diary. That works while the sign-in page has no Register now link. Register now appears only after a site administrator opens registration.

Decline refuses the invitation. After accept, decline, revoke, or expiry, the link does nothing.

Everyone in the diary can read its entries and add entries. You can change the wording only of entries you wrote. A shared entry shows the writer's name. An entry that is only in someone else's private diary does not appear for you, including on the calendar. Day is where you read a day. Privacy says who else can read an entry.

On Members, the Owner can choose Make Editor or Make Contributor. That does not change who owns the diary. An Editor cannot change roles.

Remove takes someone out of this diary. The Owner can remove an Editor or a Contributor. An Editor can remove a Contributor. Nobody can remove the Owner. The removed person's other diaries stay as they are. Their entries leave this diary. An entry that is already in another diary they still belong to stays there. An entry that would otherwise have nowhere they can read it goes into a private diary with this diary's name. If they already have a private diary with that name, it goes there.

Leave does the same with your own entries. It is on your row. The Owner cannot leave until someone else accepts ownership.

Transfer ownership is on Members, for the Owner. Choose a member, or enter an email. The page says the same thing whether or not that email already has an account. One transfer can be waiting. Cancel withdraws it. The email has a link. The person signs in as that email and chooses Accept or Decline. The link does not create an account, and it does not expire. After they accept, they are the Owner and you are an Editor, so you can then leave.

Transfer this entry, on an entry you wrote, offers that entry to an email. The email does not include the wording. After they accept, they can change it, and your name stays on record as the person who first wrote it. If the entry was only in a diary they cannot read, it moves to a diary of theirs. You can propose this after you leave, from the private diary that holds the entry. Uncheck one diary and check another, then save, to move an entry without giving it to someone else. That does not change who currently owns it.

On someone else's entry, an Owner or an Editor can choose Remove from and the diary's name. That takes the entry out of this diary and leaves it in any other diary it belongs to. If this was its only diary, the entry is deleted. Delete entry on an entry you wrote still removes it from every diary.
